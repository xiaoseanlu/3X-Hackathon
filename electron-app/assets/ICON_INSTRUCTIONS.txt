Place your menu bar icon here.

Required files:
  menubar-icon.png   — 16x16px (or 32x32 @2x retina), PNG, white/transparent
                       Used in the macOS menu bar tray.
  app-icon.icns      — macOS app icon for the .dmg installer.
                       Generate from a 1024x1024 PNG using:
                       iconutil -c icns <iconset_folder>
